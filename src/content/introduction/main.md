I am a Research Scientist at Amazon Lab126, where I work on long-term memory for language agents with [Dr. Deepak Gupta](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Nsxpe_kAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) and [Sankalp Dayal](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=ihB7aigAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). My research focuses on understanding and improving how language agents store, retrieve, and manage information over long horizons. Specifically, I worked on analyzing failure modes in episodic memory systems. Through systematic analysis, I developed a taxonomy of failure modes. I used these findings to design process reward models for training policies based on small open-weight language models for memory retrieval and management.

Prior to this, I completed my MS by Research at [CMInDS, IIT Bombay](https://www.minds.iitb.ac.in/), where I was fortunate to work with [Prof. Sunita Sarawagi](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~sunita/) on in-context learning and adaptation of foundation models for time series. I was also fortunate to collaborate with [Dr. Praneeth Netrapalli](https://praneethnetrapalli.org/), where I studied in-context learning in transformers under controlled numerical settings.

I am broadly interested in how agents can learn from experience and adapt over time. I am particularly interested in understanding how different components of agent systems, including memory, learning and feedback mechanism, and reasoning interact to enable continual improvement. I am also intrested in topics related to improving the capabilities of small open-weight language models.
