Hi, I'm Afrin

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

I am a Research Scientist at Amazon Lab126, where I work on long-term memory for language agents with Dr. Deepak Gupta and Sankalp Dayal. My research focuses on understanding and improving how language agents store, retrieve, and manage information over long horizons. Specifically, I worked on analyzing failure modes in episodic memory systems. Through systematic analysis, I developed a taxonomy of failure modes. I used these findings to design process reward models for training policies based on small open-weight language models for memory retrieval and management.

Prior to this, I completed my MS by Research at CMInDS, IIT Bombay, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Sunita Sarawagi on in-context learning and adaptation of foundation models for time series. I was also fortunate to collaborate with Dr. Praneeth Netrapalli, where I studied in-context learning in transformers under controlled numerical settings.

I am broadly interested in how agents can learn from experience and adapt over time. I am particularly interested in understanding how different components of agent systems, including memory, learning and feedback mechanisms, and reasoning interact to enable continual improvement. I am also interested in topics related to improving the capabilities of small open-weight language models.

Research interests: Agent Memory, Continual Learning, Reasoning, Learning from Feedback
