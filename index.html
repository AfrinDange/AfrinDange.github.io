Hi! I am a final-year MS by Research student at Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science, IIT Bombay, where I work as a research assistant for Prof. Sunita Sarawagi. My research primarily focuses on foundation models, lightweight adaptation of foundation models and time series modeling.

I am eager to pursue a PhD and want to build a compelling profile for top programs. To continue exploring new problems, I am actively seeking research opportunities as a Research Assistant or Predoctoral Researcher.

My research interests lie in generalizable foundation models and improving pretrained networks through lightweight adaptation. I am broadly interested in representation learning, time series modeling, multimodality, reasoning, and information retrieval.
